Hype Chain Giveaway #001 — Official Rules (Pre-Launch Review)

Pre-launch review copy — not yet effective. These rules reflect the configured giveaway flow but require final sponsor approval and applicable legal/privacy review before public activation.

1. Sponsor

Armanie Distributions Inc., operating as Limitless Expressions 3D (“Sponsor”). Contact: support@limitlessexpressions3d.com.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the 48 contiguous United States and the District of Columbia who are at least 18 years old at the time of entry. Void where prohibited. Employees of Sponsor and their immediate household members are ineligible.

3. Entry period

The entry period begins when Sponsor activates Giveaway #001 and ends exactly seven days later. The entry page will display the actual start and end timestamps once activated. Entries received outside that period are ineligible.

4. How to enter

During the entry period, submit one free entry using the online form at giveaway.html and confirm the email address using the verification link. Limit one entry per person. No purchase, payment, TikTok follow, LIVE viewing, or social media interaction is required. Such activities do not improve the odds of winning.

5. Prize

One winner receives one custom 3D-printed hype chain, approximate retail value $40, with standard shipping paid by Sponsor to an eligible US address. Customization is subject to reasonable production limits and Sponsor approval; no cash alternative except where required by law.

6. Winner selection and notification

At least 24 hours after the entry period closes, one potential winner will be selected at random from eligible, email-verified entries. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries. Sponsor will notify the potential winner by email. The potential winner must respond within 72 hours of notification and confirm eligibility and a valid shipping address. If the potential winner does not respond by the deadline or is ineligible, Sponsor may document the outcome and select an alternate at random from remaining eligible entries. Public identification of a winner will require their permission.

7. Privacy

Entry email addresses are used to verify eligibility, administer the giveaway, and contact the winner. Optional marketing consent is separate from entry and is not required. See our privacy policy. Sponsor must finalize its data-retention and deletion schedule before launch.

8. General terms

Sponsor may disqualify fraudulent or automated entries, subject to applicable law. Sponsor is not responsible for technical interruptions outside its reasonable control. Applicable federal, state, and local laws govern. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with TikTok.

Before launch: confirm sponsor approval, applicable state-specific legal and tax requirements, and the privacy data-retention/deletion schedule. This pre-launch copy is not a substitute for legal review.
