Hype Chain Giveaway #001 — Official Rules

1. Sponsor

Armanie Distributions Inc., operating as Limitless Expressions 3D (“Sponsor”). Contact: support@limitlessexpressions3d.com.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the 48 contiguous United States who are at least 18 years old at the time of entry. Void where prohibited. Employees of Sponsor and their immediate household members are ineligible.

3. Entry period

The promotional milestone is 100 TikTok followers for @limitless_expressions_3d. The entry period and official closing deadline will be announced before activation. Reaching the follower milestone does not automatically close the entry form; Sponsor will announce and administer the closing time under the published terms.

4. How to enter

During the entry period, submit one free entry using the online form at giveaway.html and confirm the email address using the verification link. Limit one entry per person. No purchase or payment is required. Following @limitless_expressions_3d on TikTok is encouraged, but does not increase the odds of selection.

5. Prize

One winner receives one custom 3D-printed hype chain, approximate retail value $40, with standard shipping paid by Sponsor to an eligible US address. If the selected winner also follows @limitless_expressions_3d on Instagram, Sponsor intends to include an additional surprise 3D-printed item, subject to the final published bonus terms. Customization is subject to reasonable production limits and Sponsor approval; no cash alternative except where required by law.

6. Winner selection and notification

At least 24 hours after the entry period closes, one potential winner will be selected at random from eligible, email-verified entries. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries. Sponsor will notify the potential winner by email. The potential winner must respond within 72 hours of notification and confirm eligibility and a valid shipping address. If the potential winner does not respond by the deadline or is ineligible, Sponsor may document the outcome and select an alternate at random from remaining eligible entries. Public identification of a winner will require their permission.

7. Privacy

Entry email addresses are used to verify eligibility, administer the giveaway, and contact the winner. Optional marketing consent is separate from entry and is not required. See our privacy policy.

8. General terms

Sponsor may disqualify fraudulent or automated entries, subject to applicable law. Sponsor is not responsible for technical interruptions outside its reasonable control. Applicable federal, state, and local laws govern. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with TikTok.
