Hype Chain Giveaway #001 — Draft Official Rules

Draft for review — not yet effective. These rules must be reviewed, finalized, and published before entries are enabled.

1. Sponsor

Armanie Distributions Inc., operating as Limitless Expressions 3D (“Sponsor”). Contact: support@limitlessexpressions3d.com.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the 48 contiguous United States and the District of Columbia who are at least 18 years old at the time of entry. Void where prohibited. Employees of Sponsor and their immediate household members are ineligible.

3. Entry period

The entry period begins on November 5, 2026, at the actual time Sponsor enables public entries, which will be displayed on the entry page. The final deadline is November 19, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Central Standard Time (CST), unless the early-close procedure applies. If Sponsor's public TikTok account @limitless_expressions_3d reaches at least 100 followers before that deadline, Sponsor will record the date and time it first verifies that milestone and publicly announce a final 24-hour entry window. Entries will close 24 hours after that recorded verification, or at the November 19 deadline, whichever is earlier. The verified milestone time and resulting exact closing timestamp must be published on the entry page promptly; no entry closes immediately upon reaching the goal. Followers are not required for entry and do not improve winning odds. Entries received after the applicable closing time are ineligible.

4. How to enter

During the entry period, submit one free entry using the online form at giveaway.html and confirm the email address using the verification link. Limit one entry per person. No purchase, payment, TikTok follow, LIVE viewing, or social media interaction is required. Such activities do not improve the odds of winning.

5. Prize

One winner receives one custom 3D-printed hype chain, approximate retail value $40, with standard shipping paid by Sponsor to an eligible US address. Winner may request a name, colors, and a reasonable logo or design. All customization is subject to Sponsor approval, intellectual-property rights, and reasonable 3D-printing and production limitations; no cash alternative except where required by law.

6. Winner selection and notification

The drawing is scheduled for 24 hours after the applicable closing timestamp, with the exact timestamp and time zone displayed on the entry page as soon as the closing timestamp is determined. If an operational delay occurs, the drawing will occur as soon as reasonably practicable thereafter, without reopening entries. One potential winner will be selected randomly from eligible, email-verified entries. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries. Sponsor will notify the potential winner privately by email. The winner's name, likeness, and customized prize will not be publicized without their separate permission. The potential winner must respond within 72 hours of notification and confirm eligibility and a valid shipping address. If the potential winner fails to respond within 72 hours after notification or is ineligible, Sponsor will randomly select an alternate from the remaining eligible, verified entries, excluding previously selected and disqualified entries. Each alternate has 72 hours after notification to respond. Sponsor will document each selection and notification.

7. Privacy

Entry email addresses are used to verify eligibility, administer the giveaway, and contact the winner. Optional marketing consent is separate from entry and is not required. See our privacy policy. Giveaway-only participant data will be deleted 90 days after the final winner is confirmed, except where retention is required by law or reasonably necessary for disputes or compliance. Separate optional marketing subscriptions are managed independently and may be unsubscribed from.

8. General terms

Sponsor may disqualify fraudulent or automated entries, subject to applicable law. Sponsor is not responsible for technical interruptions outside its reasonable control. Applicable federal, state, and local laws govern. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with TikTok.

Do not activate until these checks are complete.
