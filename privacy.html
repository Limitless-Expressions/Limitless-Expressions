Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Limitless Expressions is a brand owned and operated by Armanie Distributions Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Information we collect

When you contact us or place a request or order, we may receive your name, contact details, shipping information, customization details, uploaded artwork, and order information. Payment information is handled through our payment provider, Stripe; we do not ask you to send card details by email.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, produce and deliver orders, process transactions, provide customer service, and maintain business records.

Service providers

We may share information with providers involved in payment processing, website hosting, order fulfillment, shipping, and related business operations as needed to provide our services.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for orders, support, business records, and legal obligations. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Giveaway entries

When you enter a giveaway, we collect your email address, whether you accepted the official rules, your email-verification status, and any separate optional marketing consent. We use this information to verify entries, prevent duplicate or abusive submissions, select and contact winners, and administer the giveaway. If selected, we may request eligibility and shipping details to deliver the prize. No purchase or marketing consent is required to enter.

We retain non-winning giveaway entry records for up to 90 days after the giveaway ends, then delete them unless a longer period is required by law or reasonably necessary to resolve a dispute or investigate fraud. Winner and prize-fulfillment records may be retained longer as needed for business, tax, and legal obligations. Opting in to marketing is separate from entering the giveaway; marketing preferences and records are managed separately.

Your choices

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact Support@limitlessexpressions3d.com. Some records may need to be retained for legal or operational reasons.

Updates

We may update this policy as our services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Business contact
